Listen to French sentences on repeat

How to learn fastest with this
  1. Learn a new lesson 2–3 times. Say each sentence out loud in the pause.
  2. Switch to Recall. You hear English and try to say the French before it plays. Keep going until you get it every time.
  3. Put it on French only in the background: walking, gym, falling asleep.
  4. Once a few lessons are in, play Tout mélangé on Recall every day so old sentences keep coming back.
  5. Do the conversations after the matching lessons. Hearing a whole exchange is closer to real life.
